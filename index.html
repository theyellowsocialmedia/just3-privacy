Privacy Policy – Just 3

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Just 3 is made by The Yellow Social Media ("we").

What we collect

Nothing. Just 3 does not collect, store, or share any personal information with us or anyone else.

Microphone

The app asks for microphone access only so you can record voice notes on your tasks. Recording happens only when you tap Record. Voice notes are saved as files in the app's private storage on your phone and are never uploaded or shared.

Calendar

If you tap "Sync with Google Calendar", Just 3 reads upcoming events from your phone's calendar and adds your tasks to it. This uses the calendar storage on your phone; your calendar app then syncs it with your own Google account as usual. Just 3 itself does not connect to the internet and never sends your calendar or tasks to us. Calendar access is only used when you tap Sync, and to check whether Google Calendar already has a reminder for a task.

Notifications and alarms

Used only to remind you about your own deadlines.

Where your tasks are kept

Only on your own phone, in the app's private storage. The app does not have internet permission, so it cannot send your data anywhere. Your tasks, notes and voice notes are not included in cloud backups.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all your tasks, notes and voice notes. You can also delete any task inside the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this same link.

Contact
